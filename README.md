# Customer Shopping Behaviour Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses customer shopping behaviour to understand purchasing patterns, customer preferences, product performance, discount usage, and subscription trends.

The analysis uses Python for data exploration and preparation, PostgreSQL for querying and analysing customer data, and Power BI for creating an interactive dashboard.

The project aims to turn customer shopping data into meaningful insights that can help businesses understand their customers and make better business decisions.

## Business Problem

A retail company wants to better understand its customers' shopping behaviour to improve sales, customer satisfaction, and long-term loyalty.

The analysis focuses on customer demographics, purchasing patterns, product categories, discounts, customer reviews, shipping preferences, and subscription behaviour.

**Main Business Question:**

How can the company use customer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?

## Dataset Information

- **Dataset:** Customer Shopping Behavior
- **CSV File:** `customer_shopping_behavior.csv`
- **Total Records:** 3,900
- **Original Columns:** 18

The dataset contains customer information and shopping details, including:

- Customer demographics (Age and Gender)
- Purchased products and product categories
- Purchase amounts
- Discounts and promotional code usage
- Review ratings
- Subscription status
- Shipping types
- Payment methods
- Purchase frequency
- Shopping seasons and locations

## Project Workflow

### 1. Python – Data Exploration & Preparation

Used Python and Pandas to explore and prepare the customer shopping dataset.

- Imported and explored the dataset.
- Checked dataset information and statistical summaries.
- Identified missing values in the Review Rating column.
- Applied category-wise median imputation as a missing-value treatment.
- Standardized column names into lowercase and replaced spaces with underscores.
- Created age groups using quartile-based segmentation.
- Converted purchase frequency into days using a mapping dictionary.
- Compared discount and promotional code columns.
- Removed the redundant `promo_code_used` column.

### 2. PostgreSQL – SQL Data Analysis

Used SQL queries to analyse customer behaviour and answer 10 business questions.

- Analysed total revenue generated by male and female customers.
- Identified customers who used discounts but spent above the average purchase amount.
- Found the top five products based on average customer review ratings.
- Compared average purchase amounts between Standard and Express shipping.
- Compared average spending and total revenue between subscribers and non-subscribers.
- Identified products with the highest percentage of discounted purchases.
- Segmented customers into New, Returning, and Loyal categories based on previous purchases.
- Identified the top three most purchased products within each category.
- Analysed subscription status among repeat buyers.
- Evaluated revenue contribution across different age groups.

### 3. Power BI – Dashboard Development

Developed an interactive Customer Shopping Behaviour Dashboard to explore customer and purchasing trends.

**Key Dashboard Components:**

- KPI Cards:
  - Number of Customers
  - Average Purchase Amount
  - Average Review Rating

- Visualizations:
  - Customer Subscription Status
  - Revenue by Product Category
  - Customer ID aggregation by Product Category
  - Revenue by Age Group
  - Customer ID aggregation by Age Group

- Interactive Filters:
  - Gender
  - Product Category
  - Subscription Status
  - Shipping Type

The dashboard allows users to explore customer information and compare purchasing patterns using interactive filters.

## Key Business Questions

1. What is the total revenue generated by male and female customers?
2. Which customers used discounts but still spent more than the average purchase amount?
3. Which five products have the highest average review ratings?
4. How do average purchase amounts differ between Standard and Express shipping?
5. Do subscribed customers spend more than non-subscribers?
6. Which five products have the highest percentage of purchases with discounts?
7. How can customers be segmented into New, Returning, and Loyal categories?
8. What are the top three most purchased products within each category?
9. Are customers with more than five previous purchases more likely to subscribe?
10. Which age groups contribute the most revenue?
